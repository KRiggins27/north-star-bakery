About North Star Bakery

Our Story

With humble beginnings, surrounded by family that shared my vision, the North Star Bakery was born. We started in a small suite in our hometown starting with minimal equipment and staff but a lot of ambition, the familiy busisness eventually grew out of the tiny suite into the brick and morter that you see today. The current location is over 10,000 square feet and it is located in a properous part of the city where we make our goods fresh daily for our beloved community.

Sourcing Philosophy

We source farm to table whenever possible. The eggs, dairy and seasonal fruits are from local farmers and growers. No artifical preservatives and only organic ingredients are used. All becase we support local buisnesses.,
